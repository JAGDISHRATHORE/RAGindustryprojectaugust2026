# Privacy Notice for Loan Applicants

## 1. Controller

Nordwind Kreditbank AG is the controller for the processing of your personal
data. You can reach our data protection officer at
privacy@nordwind-kreditbank.example.

## 2. Automated decision-making

We use automated scoring when we assess loan applications. Under Article 22
GDPR you have the right to obtain human intervention, to express your point of
view and to contest the decision. A loan officer reviews every application on
request.

## 3. Retention

We keep application data for ten years after the end of the contract in line
with commercial and tax law.

## 4. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data
portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a
supervisory authority.
